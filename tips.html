Cybersecurity Safety Tips 🛡️

Learn how to protect yourself in the digital world.

🔐 Password Safety

Use strong and unique passwords for every account.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication.

Never share your passwords with others.

📧 Email Security

Avoid clicking suspicious email links.

Never download unknown attachments.

Verify the sender before responding.

📱 Social Media Safety

Keep your social media accounts private.

Do not share personal information publicly.

Avoid accepting unknown friend requests.

📲 Mobile Security

Install applications from trusted sources.

Keep your phone software updated.

Use a screen lock on your smartphone.

🌐 Safe Internet Browsing

Visit trusted websites.

Avoid suspicious download links.

Check website addresses before entering information.

🏦 Online Banking Safety

Never share your OTP or PIN.

Avoid banking through public Wi-Fi.

Check your bank transactions regularly.
